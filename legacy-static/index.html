Where do I find the right size?

Look beside the upload button on the form you’re filling in. It may say “under 50 KB” or “200 × 230 pixels.” Choose that size here. FileFit checks the settings you enter; the organisation decides whether the image meets its other requirements.

Will my photo still look clear?

We keep as much detail as the file-size limit allows. Very small limits can soften an image. Always check your downloaded file before submitting it. For exact dimensions, open the extra options.

Where is my downloaded file?

Look in your device’s Downloads folder or your browser’s downloads list. On a phone, you may need to open the Files app. FileFit adds “-filefit” to the name so you can tell it apart from the original.
